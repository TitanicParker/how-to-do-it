# From Centre finding to resolving intelligibility

**A route between How to do it and Everything Fresh**

The aim is to acquire the ability to resolve an intelligibility: recover its Operative Centre, then recognise how Transformation Pattern, Completion Topology and Persistence Mode jointly describe that same achievement.

Begin with ordinary-language Centre finding. Move into dimensional resolution when you can explain and test the governing achievement. The stages below are a proposed learning route, not an automatic certification of proficiency.

## 1. Learn to see the whole achievement

Start with [Before you begin](workbook.html#before-you-begin), then work through the ten chapters of [Finding the Operative Centre](workbook.html).

For each sentence, write your own account before reading the walkthrough. Compare it with a topic summary, an account that loses a qualification, and an interpretation that adds something unsupported. Learn to explain what each account preserves or loses.

The Centre may be a dependency or distributed organisation. A grammatical subject, a main verb or a vivid phrase does not by itself identify it.

## 2. Make the Centre inspectable

Use the [reusable worksheet](workbook.html#your-reusable-centre-finding-worksheet). Answer the six questions in ordinary language:

1. What was unresolved?
2. What changed?
3. What made it sufficient?
4. Where is sufficiency located?
5. What remains achieved?
6. What would invalidate it?

Distinguish the contributions needed to make completion sufficient from the identity of what remains achieved. A necessary term can be required to establish a result without making that result an ongoing dependence through that term.

Complete the holding tests and compare “What has been established is…” with “What obtains through what is…”. Check coverage, minimality, non-invention and determinacy before confirming.

The [live confirmation protocol](https://titanicparker.github.io/everything-fresh/confirmation-protocol.html) is the research reference for this stage.

## 3. Extend the practice without opening the atlas

Try [Further Centre practice: A-011–A-025](continuing-practice.html). Each exercise gives the exact sentence and a question to investigate. A collapsed section contains the Centre recorded in Everything Fresh; open it only after making your own attempt.

The comparison accounts come from research records. They are evidence to examine, not infallible marking keys. If you disagree, identify the exact contribution, dependency or achieved identity at issue.

For a more advanced, worked discovery, try [A-033: a Centre-first challenge](continuing-practice.html#a-033-an-additional-centre-first-challenge-collaborative-research). Compare the frozen Centre and its holding account only after your own attempt. The research selects Candidate 22 with moderate confidence, but preserves the Standing/Bearing and Distributed/Focal questions; the structural questions remain worth testing.

Then choose unfamiliar sentences from the [complete 200-sentence corpus](workbook.html#appendix--the-complete-200-sentence-practice-corpus). Preserve an unassisted attempt before consulting any available record. Keep some sentences unseen for a later transfer check.

## 4. Check readiness to begin dimensional work

Ask whether you can consistently:

- Preserve scope, modality, negation and governing qualifications.
- Identify what the whole achieves without substituting its topic or grammatical form.
- Explain why serious alternative Centre accounts omit something or invent something.
- Distinguish what completes the understanding from how the achieved result holds.
- Choose a small diagnostic change and explain precisely what it defeats.
- Recognise an unresolved interpretation and revise your account with reasons.

These checks guide review. Reading the examples, matching their wording or completing a fixed number of sentences does not establish competence. Where possible, compare unfamiliar attempts with a reviewer who can practise the method; preserve both accounts and the reasons for any correction.

## 5. Learn the axes through the confirmed achievement

Once Centre recovery is sufficiently dependable, follow the research chapters on [Transformation](https://titanicparker.github.io/everything-fresh/06-transformations.html), [Topology](https://titanicparker.github.io/everything-fresh/07-topologies.html) and [Persistence](https://titanicparker.github.io/everything-fresh/08-persistence.html).

Ask what unresolvedness changes, what constitutes its sufficiency, and how that same achievement remains available. The three dimensions constrain one completion simultaneously. Although you may study their distinctions in turn, resolution must recover one coherent conjunction.

Compare closely neighbouring achievements. Several nouns do not establish Distributed completion; one physical bearer does not establish Focal completion. Negation does not itself establish Release. A relation in a sentence does not automatically establish Bearing. Possible usefulness does not itself establish Efficacy.

The [54-cell atlas](https://titanicparker.github.io/everything-fresh/09-atlas.html) is a later reference. The [canonical control file](https://github.com/TitanicParker/everything-fresh/blob/main/THE_54_CONTROL.md) governs definitions, numbering and canonical names. Those names should check a derivation, not prompt the Centre retrospectively.

## 6. Complete the passage into research

For a full resolution, preserve the complete Centre Confirmation Record, then declare the protocol's threshold:

> OPERATIVE CENTRE CONFIRMED — SOURCE SENTENCE CLOSED.

Use the frozen record and canonical definitions for dimensional resolution. Do not return to a phrase in the original sentence to settle a classification dispute. If a defect requires renewed source analysis, preserve a separately identified revision.

Only after your own attempt, open the corresponding [sentence receipt](https://titanicparker.github.io/everything-fresh/sentence-receipts.html). Read its current status and commentary, then follow its full-diligence link. Compare the Centre before comparing coordinates. A contested result is a particular unresolved structural distinction to investigate, not a demand to guess a preferred label.

Use the [research workflow](https://titanicparker.github.io/everything-fresh/WORKFLOW.html) and [current register](https://titanicparker.github.io/everything-fresh/RESOLUTION_REGISTER.html) when moving from learning exercises to research records.
