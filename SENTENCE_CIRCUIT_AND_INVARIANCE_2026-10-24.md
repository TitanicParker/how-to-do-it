## 1. Sentence as circuit — proposed reading discipline

Treat the sentence as a time-ordered stream of constraint-bearing components rather than choose a cell by resemblance. As the sentence unfolds, inspect what each unit makes available, preserves, restricts, discharges, or closes. Examples of component functions include establishing a referent, introducing modality (*can*), quantifying a class (*every*), assigning an effect, binding a contrast (*but*, *without*, *even when*), and setting a governing condition. These are **candidate circuit components, not automatic cell triggers**.

For each successive unit, maintain an account of: (a) which completions are still possible; (b) which are blocked; (c) what remains conditional or carried forward; and (d) whether the latest contribution merely specifies the matter of an assertion or changes the sentence's governing completion. Run a counterfactual removal test on the pivotal unit. At the end, freeze a **single whole-sentence Operative Centre** and derive Transformation × Topology × Persistence from it, not separately from prominent words or the described object's real-world action.

A circuit trace is an **experimental operationalization**, not a demonstrated mapping from English components to 54 cell circuits. A cell number must never be used to reverse-engineer the trace.

## 3. Cross-language same-sentence stacking — complementary experiment

**Hypothesis:** a faithful translation can change word order, morphology and phrasing while preserving the original sentence's governing operative completion and thereby its 54-cell classification.

**Two different procedures:** (1) *Invariant-guided translation* uses the source Centre as a constraint to preserve its achievement; (2) *invariant-blind verification* asks independent resolvers, without seeing the target cell, to trace the translated versions and identify their own Centres and coordinates. Agreement produced merely by prompting translators with the desired number is circular evidence. Check scope, modality, conditionals, negation and qualification before treating different cell outputs as failures of invariance.

Same-cell stacking varies meaning while holding a **proposed cell** constant. Same-sentence multilingual stacking varies linguistic expression while trying to hold the **governing intended achievement** constant. They test different claims.
